# Discussion Playbook

## Role

Interpret results, boundaries, limitations, and implications without expanding claims beyond evidence.

## Required Moves

1. Summarize what the results support.
2. Explain where the method helps.
3. Identify failure modes and limitations.
4. State implications for future work.

## Checks

- Do not convert speculation into conclusion.
- Explicitly separate limitations from future work.
- Preserve negative and inconclusive outcomes.
