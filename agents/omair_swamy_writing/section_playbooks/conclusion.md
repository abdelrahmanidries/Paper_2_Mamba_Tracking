# Conclusion Playbook

## Role

Close the paper by restating the problem, method, evidence-supported findings, and limitations in compact form.

## Required Moves

1. Restate the addressed problem.
2. Summarize the proposed method.
3. State main verified results.
4. Mention important limitations or scope.
5. End with a restrained implication.

## Checks

- No new citations or claims.
- No new results.
- No broader novelty than the Introduction established.
