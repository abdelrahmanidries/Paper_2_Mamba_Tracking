# Reviewer Response Playbook

## Role

Draft respectful, specific responses that distinguish reviewer-facing explanation from manuscript changes.

## Required Structure

1. `Reviewer comment`: quote or summarize the concern.
2. `Response`: acknowledge, explain, and answer with evidence.
3. `Manuscript change`: state exactly what was changed or why no change was made.
4. `Verification`: point to experiment, citation, table, or section.

## Checks

- Do not promise changes that were not made.
- Do not argue defensively.
- Preserve exact numeric results and citation keys.
