# Related Work Rubric

Score each item from 0 to 2.

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Family organization | Chronological list | Partial grouping | Clear functional families |
| Prior progress | Dismissive | Acknowledged unevenly | Balanced progress and limits |
| Evidence boundaries | Unsupported negatives | Some caution | Explicit/implicit limits separated |
| Citation coverage | Sparse | Adequate | Every family supported |
| Present-work positioning | Overclaims | Broad | Narrow and evidence-backed |
| Domain transfer control | Cross-domain claims | Some leakage | Claims stay within evidence |

Recommended minimum for release: 10/12.
