# General Writing Rubric

Score each item from 0 to 2.

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Evidence grounding | Unsupported claims | Mixed support | All major claims traced |
| Citation coverage | Missing citations | Some weak coverage | Citations cover prior work and claims |
| Scientific preservation | Meaning changed | Minor ambiguities | Meaning preserved |
| IEEE structure | Unstructured | Partly organized | Clear paragraph roles |
| Style fit | Generic | Some fit | Controlled Ahmad-Swamy/IEEE tone |
| Novelty caution | Overclaims | Mostly cautious | Strictly bounded |
| Readability | Hard to follow | Adequate | Clear and concise |

Recommended minimum for release: 11/14 with no zero in evidence grounding or scientific preservation.
