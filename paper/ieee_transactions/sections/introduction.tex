\section{Introduction}

RGB object tracking must maintain target localization under appearance change, motion, and image-quality degradation. Synthetic and real corruptions such as blur, low resolution, and Gaussian noise can weaken template-search matching, response-map localization, and bounding-box regression. Recent Mamba and state-space models have been explored in vision, while restoration-oriented variants such as MambaIR and MambaIRv2 adapt state-space modeling to image restoration~\cite{guo2024mambair,guo2025mambairv2}. This paper evaluates a narrower question: whether restoration-oriented state-space feature adaptation can improve target-discriminative tracking features without changing the overall \baseline{} tracking architecture.

The goal is not to claim that Mamba is new to tracking, nor that degradation-robust tracking is unsolved. InvTrack is direct local evidence that degradation-invariant RGB tracking can be formulated through clean/degraded training, feature consistency, and response-map fusion~\cite{invtrack_local}. Instead, we study a specific missing intersection: restoration-oriented state-space feature adaptation inside RGB template-search tracking. The final method is intentionally conservative. It freezes the \baseline{} backbone, trains \method{} and the box head, and uses global clean--degraded feature consistency. This makes the empirical question testable while reducing the risk of conflating the proposed module with broad architecture changes.

The contributions of this Paper Freeze V1 manuscript are:
\begin{itemize}
    \item a conservative \baseline{}+\method{} adaptation with frozen backbone and trainable \method{} plus box head;
    \item an evaluation under clean, motion-blur, low-resolution, and Gaussian-noise conditions on selected OTB, UAV123, and corrected NFS subsets;
    \item a corrected NFS provenance path that replaces invalid XYXY-as-XYWH NFS metrics with normalized aligned XYWH annotations;
    \item a same-GPU efficiency and parameter comparison on Tesla V100-PCIE-32GB hardware;
    \item a cautious claim boundary that reports positive OTB/UAV123 results, mixed corrected NFS transfer, and rejected negative ablations.
\end{itemize}
